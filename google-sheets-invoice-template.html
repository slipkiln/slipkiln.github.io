Free Google Sheets invoice template

A clean, printable invoice with automatic totals and tax, plus a ledger tab for making many invoices at once. Free download, no sign-up.

Download the template (.xlsx) Download .csv

Open it in Google Sheets with File → Import → Upload, or in Excel. The .csv is the ledger tab only.

What's in the template

  • Invoice tab: a one-page invoice. Type your business details, the client and up to 10 line items; Amount, Subtotal, Tax and Total calculate themselves. Print area is set to one page.
  • Invoices tab: one line item per row with the columns Invoice #, Date, Due date, Client, Client email, Client address, Description, Qty, Unit price, Tax % and Notes. Rows that share an invoice number are one invoice. This is the tab you use to make many PDFs at once.

How to use it

  1. Download the .xlsx above. In Google Sheets choose File → Import → Upload, pick the file and choose Create new spreadsheet. (It also opens in Excel.)
  2. On the Invoice tab, replace the sample business and client details. Change the tax rate in the totals box (use 0 if you don't charge tax).
  3. Save it as a PDF: File → Download → PDF, then attach it to your email.
  4. For the next invoice, change the invoice number, date and line items. Keep a copy of each one so your records match what you sent.

Tips for invoices that get paid

  • Use a unique, increasing invoice number (INV-1001, INV-1002, …). Your client's accounts team will ask for it.
  • Always show a due date and the payment terms (for example, “Net 30”).
  • List how to pay you: bank details, a payment link, or a mailing address for checks.
  • Itemize. “Consulting, 10 hours at $120” gets approved faster than “Services: $1,200”.

FAQ

Is the template free? Yes, with no sign-up and no watermark on the template.

How do I save a Google Sheets invoice as a PDF? On the Invoice tab, choose File → Download → PDF. For bulk PDFs, use the Invoices tab with the add-on (coming soon).

Can one invoice have several line items? Yes. In the Invoices tab, put each item on its own row and repeat the invoice number.
