Questions

What counts as a document?

One successful render that returns a PDF. Failed renders are free.

What if I go over my plan?

Paid plans keep working and extra documents are billed at the overage rate at the end of the month. Free stops at 50 until next month.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes. Switch plans or cancel any time from Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period. See the refund policy.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe. Prices are in USD and exclude any applicable taxes.
