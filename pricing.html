FAQ

What counts as a document?

One successful render call producing one PDF, up to 20 pages. Failed renders are not billed.

What happens if I go over my plan?

Paid plans continue at the overage rate shown, billed at the end of the month. The Free plan stops rendering at 50 documents until next month.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from the billing portal. See our refund policy.

Payment methods

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Prices in USD, exclusive of any applicable taxes.
