Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

DJ Solutions LLC ("we") operates SlipKiln. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, company name, API keys, branding assets you upload.
  • Billing data: handled by Stripe; we receive plan, invoice and limited card details (brand, last 4), never full card numbers.
  • Usage data: request counts, timestamps, sizes and error logs needed for billing and reliability.
  • Document content: data you send to render. It is processed in memory to produce the PDF, which is returned to you; we do not keep document content or generated PDFs after delivery (at most 24 hours in transient caches or logs on error).

Google Sheets add-on and Google user data

Our Google Sheets add-on (Invoice PDF Generator) accesses the following Google user data, only to provide its features:

  • Your Google account email address, to create your free SlipKiln account and count your monthly PDF allowance. To confirm the address, the add-on sends us a short-lived Google sign-in token once; we check it with Google and do not store it.
  • The rows of the spreadsheet you have open, only when you click Generate, to build the invoice or receipt data. That data is sent to our API to render the PDF, processed in memory and not stored after the PDF is returned.
  • Files the add-on creates in your Google Drive (a "SlipKiln Invoices" folder and the PDFs in it), to save your PDFs and, only if you turn the option on, share a PDF view-only with your client's email address. The add-on cannot see other files in your Drive, and it has no access to your Gmail.

From the add-on we store your email address, an API key (stored hashed) and monthly PDF counts. We do not store spreadsheet contents or PDFs. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone except the service providers listed below as needed to run the service, for security, or to comply with law. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models, and no person at SlipKiln reads it unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

SlipKiln's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To delete your add-on data, uninstall the add-on and email support@slipkiln.com from the same address; we delete the account within 30 days. You can revoke the add-on's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What we don't do

We do not sell personal data, use advertising trackers, or use your document content to train models. This website sets no tracking cookies.

Processors

We use a small set of service providers: Stripe (payments), our hosting provider (servers in the EU/US), and a transactional email provider. They process data only on our instructions.

Retention

Account and billing records are kept while your account is active and as required for tax and accounting law. Logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal data by emailing support@slipkiln.com. Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK GDPR, California, Colorado Privacy Act) you may have additional rights.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). API keys are stored hashed. Rendering runs in isolated, network-restricted workers.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We will post updates here and notify account holders of material changes.
